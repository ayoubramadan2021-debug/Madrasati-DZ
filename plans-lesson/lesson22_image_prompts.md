## Prompt 1
Create a vertical 3D cartoon educational scene inside a school nutrition lab in an Algerian primary school. Teacher Taline wearing a soft pink hijab and modest outfit welcomes three children named Fadel Rahma and Sirine. They stand near a large lab table showing a variety of foods including fruits vegetables bread milk yogurt fish or chicken and a small amount of sweets placed aside. The children look curious and happy. Warm child friendly atmosphere clean colorful classroom lab. Leave calm empty space at the bottom for karaoke captions. No text.

## Prompt 2
Create a vertical 3D cartoon educational scene in an Algerian school nutrition lab. Teacher Taline shows a balanced and diverse plate on a demonstration table. The plate clearly contains vegetables fruits grains such as bread or rice dairy such as milk or yogurt and protein such as fish or chicken. Fadel Rahma and Sirine watch carefully and interact with the food display. Warm friendly lighting highly readable composition for young children. Leave calm empty space at the bottom for karaoke captions. No text.

## Prompt 3
Create a vertical 3D cartoon scene in the nutrition lab of an Algerian primary school. Teacher Taline explains that healthy diverse food gives strength and energy. Show Fadel looking energetic and active after a healthy meal while Rahma holds a bottle of water and Sirine smiles beside fruits and healthy foods. The feeling is lively positive and educational. Keep the background as a clean school nutrition lab. Leave calm empty space at the bottom for karaoke captions. No text.

## Prompt 4
Create a vertical 3D cartoon educational scene in an Algerian school nutrition lab. Teacher Taline compares healthy foods on one side with sugary foods on the other side. Show sweets candies cake and sugary drinks grouped together while fruits vegetables milk and bread appear as healthier choices. Fadel Rahma and Sirine observe attentively. The scene must be gentle and child friendly not scary. Leave calm empty space at the bottom for karaoke captions. No text.

## Prompt 5
Create a vertical 3D cartoon educational scene in the nutrition lab of an Algerian primary school about sugar and dental health. Teacher Taline explains gently using a large friendly tooth model and a toothbrush. Rahma looks at a happy clean tooth model while Sirine holds an apple and Fadel notices a small sweet nearby. The scene should clearly suggest that too much sugar can harm teeth but remain warm and reassuring. Leave calm empty space at the bottom for karaoke captions. No text.

## Prompt 6
Create a vertical 3D cartoon closing scene in an Algerian school nutrition lab. Teacher Taline with Fadel Rahma and Sirine happily prepare a healthy diverse plate together. Show fruits vegetables bread grains milk or yogurt and protein foods arranged nicely while a small sweet remains in the background and is not the focus. All characters look proud and happy. Bright warm educational ending scene. Leave calm empty space at the bottom for karaoke captions. No text.
